Ticket & Payment FAQ

Which passes are available?

You can choose from Normal Pass, VIP Pass, and Exclusive Pass based on the access and benefits you need.

How do I book a ticket?

Select your preferred pass, click Book Now, register with your details, verify the OTP, and continue to complete your booking.

What payment methods are accepted?

Payment options will be shown during checkout. Common options may include UPI, cards, net banking, or supported digital wallets.

Will I receive a confirmation after payment?

Yes. After successful booking, your confirmation details will be sent to the email address or phone number used during registration.

Can I change my selected pass?

You can choose a different pass before completing payment. After confirmation, pass changes may depend on availability and event policy.

Are ticket payments refundable?

Refunds, cancellations, and transfers are handled according to the event ticketing policy shared at the time of booking.

What should I do if payment fails?

If payment fails, please retry after checking your bank status. If money is debited but no confirmation is received, contact the support team with your payment reference.

Do I need to carry ID with my ticket?

Yes. Please carry a valid government or institution ID along with your booking confirmation for smooth entry at the venue.
